# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 23 November 2026

First official release, based on the user-approved 0.1.2 playtest.

- Item icons and distances, including ammo and armour crates.
- General supplies show over 10m through 60m; weapons show names within 10m.
- Hide empty limited-use crates and nearby supply markers.
- Compact HUD with five markers maximum and the accepted enlarged size.
- Frame-following marker movement, stable widget reuse and throttled item discovery.
- Integrated install/update/uninstall, English default with optional Thai on GitHub.
- Separate English-only manual package for Nexus.
